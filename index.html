Before you rent

  • Ask what the monthly rent includes, such as water, parking or internet.
  • Landlords often ask for an application, ID and proof of income.
  • A security deposit is common. Get the amount and refund terms in writing.

Rentals in India

Find flats, independent houses and studios in major Indian cities. Rents are listed in Indian rupees (₹) per month, and you deal directly with the owner.

Popular cities

Mumbai Delhi Bengaluru Pune Hyderabad Chennai

Before you rent

  • Check whether the flat is furnished, semi-furnished or unfurnished.
  • Ask about maintenance charges and who pays them.
  • Rental agreements are often made for 11 months. Read the notice period and deposit refund terms.

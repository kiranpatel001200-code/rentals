Renting on RentNest: common questions

How do I find a rental on RentNest?

Type a city, ZIP code or address, then filter by state, property type and bedrooms. Open a listing to see photos, rent, amenities and the address, and send the landlord a message.

Is RentNest only for the United States?

Yes. RentNest is for renters and landlords in the USA, and every rent is shown in US dollars per month.

How do I list my property for rent?

Create a free landlord account, tap List your property, and answer a few easy steps: type of place, address, rent, amenities and photos. Your listing goes live as soon as you publish, and you can edit, pause or delete it any time.

How do I stay safe from rental scams?

See the home in person before you pay anything, read the lease before you sign, and never wire money or send gift cards to someone you have not met. Report any listing that looks suspicious. No website can promise that every listing is perfect, so always check for yourself.

Own a property?

Create a listing in a few minutes and reach renters searching in your city. Add clear photos, the monthly rent and a short description.

1. Who we are

RentNest ("we", "us") runs this website for renters and landlords in the United States. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who sees it, and your choices. By using RentNest you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

In short: we do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with other companies for their own advertising. We only share it with the people and service providers described below, so the site can work.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number (optional), whether you are a renter or landlord, and your password. Passwords are stored in a protected (hashed) form by our login provider. We cannot read them.
  • Listings: the address, photos, rent, description, amenities and neighborhood you enter. Listings and their photos are public.
  • Messages: messages between renters and landlords, move-in dates and tour requests.
  • Reviews and reports: reviews you write (shown with the name you choose) and reports you file.
  • Payments: if you buy a Contact Pass, PayPal processes the payment. We receive the order number, amount and date. We do not receive or store your card or bank details.
  • Usage data: pages you visit and what you search for, linked to a random visitor ID stored in your browser, and to your account if you are logged in. Your browser and network also send technical data such as your IP address and device type to the companies that deliver our site.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, send login and verification emails, and run the site.
  • To show listings and let renters and landlords contact each other.
  • To send you notices, for example that you received a message (we do not include the sender's phone or email in those emails).
  • To process Contact Pass payments and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To review reports, enforce our Terms and keep people safe.
  • To understand how the site is used (for example which cities are searched) and to improve it.
  • To follow the law.

4. Who sees your information

  • Other users. When you message a landlord, they see your name, that your email is verified, and your message. If a landlord or renter has an active Contact Pass, they can see the other person's email and phone number in their messages or on the listing. Listings are public to everyone.
  • Service providers that process information for us under their own privacy terms: Supabase (database, login, file storage and server functions), GitHub Pages (website hosting), PayPal (payments), Google (we send login emails through Gmail), an email delivery service for message notices, and content delivery networks that load site code, such as jsDelivr and PayPal's own scripts.
  • Our team may view accounts, listings and messages when needed to keep the site safe, handle reports and give support.
  • Legal and safety. We may share information if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect people, the site or our rights.
  • Business changes. If RentNest is sold or merged, information may move to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

We do not sell personal information and we do not "share" it for cross-context advertising (those words have the meaning in California law). We do not run ad networks on RentNest.

5. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you logged in and to remember a random visitor ID so we can count visits and searches. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. When you pay with PayPal, PayPal may set its own cookies. Our site does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because we do not track you across other sites.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within about 30 days, except what we must keep to follow the law, handle disputes, stop fraud, or enforce our Terms (for example payment records). Deleted data can remain in secure backups for a short time.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules on our database so users can only reach what they are allowed to see, and limited staff access. No website can be 100% secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your name, phone number, email and password in My account, and edit or delete your listings in My listings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account and data. Write to . We may ask you to confirm it is you, and we reply within 45 days.
  • California and other U.S. states. If you live in California or another state with a privacy law, you may have the right to know what we collect, to access, correct and delete it, and not to be treated unfairly for using your rights. You also have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information. We do not sell or share it, so there is nothing to opt out of. You can ask someone else to make a request for you.
  • You can stop email notices from us by changing your account or writing to us. We still send account and security emails.

9. Children

RentNest is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child gave us information, write to and we will delete it.

10. Things you make public

Your listing (including the address, photos, price and description) and your reviews are visible to anyone, including people who are not logged in and search engines. Think before you post anything you do not want public.

11. Other websites

Our site may link to other websites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important we will tell you on the site or by email.

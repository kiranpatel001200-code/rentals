25 Questions to Ask a Potential Roommate Before Moving In

RentNest Editorial Team · Updated November 6, 2026 · Practical US rental guidance

A short conversation before moving in can reveal whether your expectations line up. Use these questions as a starting point; you do not need to ask them like an interview. Share your own answers too.

Money and responsibilities

1. What is the exact rent share and due date? 2. Which utilities are separate? 3. How are bills divided? 4. Is a deposit required, and who holds it? 5. What happens if someone pays late? 6. Who contacts the landlord about repairs?

Schedules and noise

7. What are your usual work or school hours? 8. Do you work from home? 9. What quiet hours feel reasonable? 10. How often do you cook? 11. Do you play music or use speakers? 12. How should we handle early mornings or late nights?

Cleaning and shared spaces

13. How often should the bathroom be cleaned? 14. How will we split kitchen cleaning? 15. What is the plan for trash and recycling? 16. Can we share groceries or household supplies? 17. Which items or spaces are private?

Guests, pets and lifestyle

18. How much notice should guests receive? 19. Are overnight guests okay, and how often? 20. Are pets allowed under the lease? 21. Does anyone smoke or vape? 22. Are parties or gatherings okay, and what limits make sense?

Plans and communication

23. How long do you plan to stay? 24. What happens if someone needs to move out early? 25. How should we raise concerns before they become arguments?

Keep in mind: Ask about lease rules before agreeing to guests, pets or subletting. A roommate’s preference cannot override the property’s lease or local law.
